---
title: "Signatures"
description: "Verify webhook signatures."
---

DocPay signs webhook deliveries so you can verify that requests were sent by DocPay and not by an attacker. Every delivery includes the `DocPay-Signature` header.

## Signature format

The `DocPay-Signature` header has the following format:

```
DocPay-Signature: t=<unix seconds>,v1=<hex HMAC-SHA256 of 't.body'>
```

- `t`: Delivery timestamp in Unix seconds.
- `v1`: Hexadecimal HMAC-SHA256 signature computed over `t.body` (the timestamp `t`, a period `.`, and the raw request body).

## Verifying signatures

To verify a webhook signature:

1. **Extract timestamp and signature**: Parse the `t` timestamp and `v1` signature from the `DocPay-Signature` header.
2. **Check timestamp tolerance**: Reject deliveries older than 300 seconds (5 minutes) to prevent replay attacks (`SIGNATURE_TOLERANCE_SECONDS = 300`).
3. **Compute expected signature**: Compute the HMAC-SHA256 of `t.body` using your webhook secret and format it in hexadecimal.
4. **Compare signatures**: Compare the computed signature to the `v1` value in the header.
