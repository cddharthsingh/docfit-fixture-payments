---
title: "Signatures"
description: "Verify webhook signatures."
---

Webhook deliveries include a `DocPay-Signature` header to verify authenticity and protect against replay attacks.

## Header format

The `DocPay-Signature` header contains a timestamp and a signature:

```http
DocPay-Signature: t=<unix seconds>,v1=<hex HMAC-SHA256 of 't.body'>
```

- `t`: The timestamp of the delivery in Unix seconds.
- `v1`: The hex HMAC-SHA256 of `t.body`.

## Verification

Reject deliveries older than 300 seconds (5 minutes) to protect against replay attacks.
